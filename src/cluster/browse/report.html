gene modules × cell modules

The gene modules and cell modules of faba cluster --model blocks. Click a pane or press Tab to focus it, then ↑/↓ to move. Choosing a gene module (left) fills the sites pane (right); Space on a site (or Shift-dragging its row down) pins its gene to the strip below, which grows a row at a time as genes are added; there the pinned genes (from any module) run side by side; Delete unpins one (or Shift-drag it off the strip; Shift-drag along it to reorder; a plain click only selects), Shift+X clears them, and Enter opens one's pileup. When the cell modules do not all fit across a grid, its columns narrow and then [/] scroll them in every grid together, those hidden counted in its corner. Each level is a margin of the next. Press / and type a gene name to jump to it.
